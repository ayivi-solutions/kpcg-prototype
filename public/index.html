About KPCG

One platform. Many climate-governance pathways.

A public digital space where national policy, county realities, civil society, knowledge and participation can meet.

KPCG is presented here as a connected climate-governance environment rather than a collection of isolated web pages.

The continuous architecture makes institutional identity, geography, thematic priorities, implementation, policy, evidence, events and participation part of one narrative. Users may still jump directly to any section, but they no longer have to repeatedly leave one page and load another simply to understand the platform.

Detailed records remain available for material that genuinely benefits from depth — programme records, county profiles, policy documents, knowledge resources, articles, events and administrative workflows.
